# Nexora Institute of Computing - Curriculum Map

A complete **8-semester, 80-subject CSE curriculum**, plus advanced courses,
alternate editions, workshops, and specialisation tracks - all built in the open.

---

## The official curriculum (80 subjects, 8 semesters)

| Semester | Theme | Subjects |
| :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **1** | Foundations (Beginner) | 10 |
| **2** | Core Programming and Structures (Beginner-Intermediate) | 10 |
| **3** | Algorithms and Systems Foundations (Intermediate) | 10 |
| **4** | Software Engineering and Networks (Intermediate-Advanced) | 10 |
| **5** | Intelligence and Security (Advanced) | 10 |
| **6** | Deep Systems and Specialisation (Advanced) | 10 |
| **7** | Capstone and Industry (Advanced-Professional) | 10 |
| **8** | Professional Launch (Professional) | 10 |
